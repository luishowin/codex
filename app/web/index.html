How to use Codex

Ask, open, read.

Your sovereign library. Millions of distilled reference pages, answered in milliseconds, with no account and nothing leaving your machine.

Step 1

Type two letters or more

Suggestions drop down as you type. Pick one or press Enter for the full results page.

Step 2

Quick or deep

Quick checks titles and summaries. Press Tab for deep, which reads the full text of every page.

Step 3

Open and follow on

Move with ↑ ↓, open with Enter. Each page lists related pages at the bottom. Esc always takes you back.

The shelf

One format. Every domain.

Every source, from a seven-million-article encyclopedia to OCR'd field manuals, lands in the same shape: doc, fts_quick, related, codex_meta. One query language across all of them.

How it's built

Rebuild, index, serve.

Rebuild

ZIM to SQLite

Archives stream through per-source adapters into SQLite: 68.5 GB of source became 33.9 GB of codex, 2.0× smaller. iFixit's picture-heavy archive shrank 25.6×.

Index

Ranked, not just matched

A link-graph pass ranks pages by importance, then two FTS tiers index titles, summaries and full text. Scores are computed at build time.

Serve

Milliseconds, locally

One daemon holds every library open and answers on 127.0.0.1:8765. No cloud, no upload, no connection. Queries land in single-digit milliseconds.
