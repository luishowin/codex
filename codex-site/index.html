How it works

      

        

          
Rebuild

          

            ZIM archives are opened read-only and streamed through per-source adapters into
            SQLite: 68.5 GB of source became 33.9 GB of codex: 2.0× smaller,
            and iFixit's picture-heavy archive shrank 25.6×. The text stays; the bloat doesn't.
          

          
zstd level 15

        

        

          
Index

          

            Pass 2 walks the link graph (PageRank over the related table) so
            ranking knows what matters. Search hits fts_quick first, then the
            deep tier for full-body matches. Prefix queries expand only the last term.
          

          
SQLite FTS5

        

        

          
Serve

          

            One daemon loads every *.codex.db and answers on
            127.0.0.1:8765: no cloud, no upload, no connection. Wikipedia takes
            ~20–30 s to load once; queries after that measure in single-digit milliseconds.
          

          
0 network calls
